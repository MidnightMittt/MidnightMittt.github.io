What this number is

It is the pool's marginal price — what the next infinitesimal swap executes at. A real trade moves it, and in a pool this thin a large trade moves it a lot: selling 7,088 PRIORS earlier today cost about 3% in impact on top of the 1% fee. So this is the right number for "what is my position worth", and an optimistic one for "what could I sell at".
