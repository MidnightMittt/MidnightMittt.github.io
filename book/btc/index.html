What this position does

It holds BTC and ETH together and earns 0.3% on every trade between them while the ratio stays inside the band. Because both legs are crypto, the ratio moves far less than either against the dollar — measured at roughly 10–15% annualised versus 300%+ for PRIORS. That is the entire reason a band this tight (±0.6%) is sensible here and was never sensible there.

The band is on the BTC/ETH ratio, not on BTC in dollars. BTC can fall while this position climbs its band — that happens whenever ETH falls faster than BTC. The dollar figures below the ratio are derived from the live ETH price and will drift every refresh even though the band itself has not moved.

It is not a bet on BTC going up. If BTC and ETH both fall together the position falls with them. What it earns on is the ratio staying put.
